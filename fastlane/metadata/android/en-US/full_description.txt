BVK Tracker is a free GPX tracker, originally built for the Blocq van Kuffeler (WSV Blocq Kuffeler) — handy for anyone who wants to record their route on the water or on the go.

🗺️ MAP LAYERS
Choose OpenStreetMap, Street Map (Esri) or OpenTopoMap, with optional OpenSeaMap sea marks layered on top.

📍 LIVE GPS TRACKING
Start/stop your track with one tap, see live distance and speed, and save your route as a standard GPX file.

🛟 YOUR RIDE IS NEVER LOST
While recording, the app keeps a recovery copy. If Android closes the app, your ride is simply in your list the next time you start. Saving never overwrites another ride.

🌬️ WEATHER AND WATER LEVEL
Wind (Beaufort and knots), temperature and the current water level at the Markermeer (Rijkswaterstaat) right on screen.

📤 EXPORT AND SHARE
Every recorded track is saved as a .gpx file, ready to share or import into other navigation apps.

🌐 SHARE LIVE (OPTIONAL)
If you switch it on yourself, your boat appears live on the BVK map at alltrexx.live: link with your boat's name and your position is shared about every 15 seconds. Turning it off or unlinking stops it immediately. When it is off, nothing is sent.

💶 FREE
No subscription, no paywall, no account needed.

Your rides stay on your own device. No account and no tracking of you as a user; only if you switch on live sharing yourself are your boat name and position sent to alltrexx.live. Weather is requested for a fixed point on the Markermeer, not for your location.
